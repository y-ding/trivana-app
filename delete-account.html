Delete your Trivana account

You can delete your account and all of its data at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Trivana and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account, then Delete Permanently.

Your account and server data are deleted immediately, and the data stored on that phone is cleared.

Option 2: by email (if you can't use the app)

Send a request from the email address you signed up with:

Email a deletion request

Or write to trivana.healthapp@gmail.com. We may ask you to confirm the request from that address. We complete requests within 30 days and email you when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and profile: name, email, password, date of birth, gender, phone number and address.
  • Onboarding and lifestyle answers, assessments and mood check-ins.
  • Therapeutic journal entries and completions, goals and check-ins.
  • Rewards, points and achievements.
  • Vana replies you reported, with the message and details you sent.
  • Data stored on your phone (sensor data, wellbeing scores, chats and journals) is removed when you delete in the app, or when you uninstall the app.

What we keep

Nothing linked to your account. Server logs and backups that may still contain your data are deleted on a rolling basis, normally within 30 days.
