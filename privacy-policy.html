Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Trivana is a mental wellness app. This policy explains what information we collect, why, where it is kept, and the choices you have. Most of the sensing data the app uses never leaves your phone.

1. Summary

  • Passive sensor data (location, physical activity, call activity, screen time, sleep and Health Connect data) is stored and processed only on your phone. It is not uploaded to our servers.
  • The Vana chat assistant runs on your phone using a downloaded AI model. Your chat messages are not sent to us or to any AI provider, unless you choose to report a Vana reply (see section 2).
  • Your account details and the information you choose to enter in certain features (listed below) are stored on our servers so the app works across sessions.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • You can delete your account and all server data at any time from Profile > Delete Account.

2. Information stored on our servers

When you create an account and use the app, we store the following on our servers:

  • Account information: first and last name, email address, a hashed (not readable) password, date of birth, gender and phone number.
  • Home address you enter during sign-up, and its map coordinates.
  • Onboarding answers: lifestyle, routine, wellbeing goals, concerns and communication preferences you choose to share.
  • Mood check-ins, including any notes and anxiety ratings you add.
  • Therapeutic journal entries and completion history.
  • Goals, goal check-ins, rewards, points and achievements.
  • Vana replies you report: the reported reply, the message you sent just before it, and the reason and any details you add. We use these only to review the report and make Vana safer.
  • Basic technical information needed to run the service, such as sign-in tokens and server logs (IP address, request time, errors).

3. Information that stays on your phone

With your permission, the app collects the following data in the background, including when the app is closed or not in use, to estimate daily wellbeing patterns (sleep, social activity, phone use and physical activity). This data is stored in the app's private storage on your device and is processed on-device. It is not uploaded to our servers.

  • Location: your location about every 5 minutes, used to work out how long you spend at home, in your dorm, and at places you save (such as where you study, exercise or meet friends). Saved places and precise location points stay on your phone.
  • Physical activity: whether you are still, walking, running, cycling or in a vehicle (Android Activity Recognition).
  • Call activity: when a phone call starts and ends, and its duration. We never read phone numbers, contacts, call history or call audio.
  • Phone usage: when the screen is unlocked and total screen-on time (Android Usage Access). We do not read the content of other apps.
  • Sleep and health data from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iOS), if you allow it: sleep sessions, steps, distance, heart rate, active calories and exercise sessions. Read only; the app never writes to Health Connect.
  • Daily wellbeing scores and forecasts calculated from the data above.
  • Your chats with Vana, gratitude and personal journal entries, and the on-device search index built from them.
  • Crisis check: Vana looks at your messages on your phone for signs that you may be in crisis, so it can show crisis resources. This check runs on your phone and nothing is sent to us.

4. Microphone

If you use voice input in the journal, the microphone is used only while you are speaking, to turn your speech into text. This uses your phone's speech recognition service. On most Android phones that is Google's service, which may send the audio to Google to convert it to text, under Google's privacy policy. Trivana does not record or keep the audio, and the app does not listen in the background.

5. How we use information

  • To provide the app's features: wellbeing insights, forecasts, journaling, goals, rewards and the chat assistant.
  • To keep your account secure, verify your email and help you reset your password.
  • To maintain, debug and improve the service.
  • To respond to your support requests.
  • We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not make decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

6. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share information only with service providers that run parts of the service for us, and only as needed:

  • Cloud hosting and database providers (our server runs on Vercel) that store the server data listed in section 2.
  • Google (Gmail), which delivers our verification and password reset emails.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim, which receives the address or place names you type (on the address screen and when saving places) so they can be converted to a map location.
  • Hugging Face, which hosts the AI model files the app downloads. Only a standard download request is made; none of your data is sent.
  • Your phone's speech recognition service, if you use voice input (see section 4).
  • We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect someone's safety.

7. Health Connect and Apple Health

Data read from Health Connect or Apple Health is used only to provide wellbeing insights inside the app, stays on your device, and is never used for advertising, sold, or shared with third parties. Our use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

8. Security

Data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Passwords are stored hashed. On-device data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and a screen lock.

9. Retention and deletion

  • Server data is kept while your account is active.
  • Delete Account (Profile > Delete Account) permanently removes your account and all associated server data, including journals, assessments, mood history, goals and rewards.
  • On-device data is removed when you delete your account in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Logging out keeps it on the phone, set aside, so it is there when you sign in again.
  • If you can't access the app, you can request deletion at https://y-ding.github.io/trivana-app/delete-account.html or by emailing trivana.healthapp@gmail.com. We complete requests within 30 days.
  • Server logs and backups are deleted on a rolling basis, normally within 30 days.

10. Your choices and rights

  • You can turn any permission off at any time in Profile > App Permissions or in your phone's settings. The app keeps working with reduced insights.
  • You can view and edit your profile in the app, and export your therapeutic journal.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, or to object to processing. Contact us at trivana.healthapp@gmail.com to make a request.

11. Children

Trivana is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Not a medical service

Trivana is a wellness tool. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any medical or mental health condition, and it is not an emergency service. If you are in crisis or may harm yourself, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line right away.

13. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our practices change. If the changes are significant, we will tell you in the app before they take effect.
